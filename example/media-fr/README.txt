petites roues - example media (French)

One-minute excerpts that go with the study documents in ../documents-fr. Each clip was cut
from the source below and re-encoded small (H.264/AAC MP4, 480x270, mono audio); document timings
start at the clip's zero. The originals were not changed.

series/peppa-pig/s01e01-les-flaques-de-boue.mp4
  Peppa Pig, Les Flaques de Boue (season 1, episode 1).
  Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mh_GJPIaPTI, 00:30.720 to 01:33.540.

series/peppa-pig/s01e02-a-la-recherche-de-mr-dinosaure.mp4
  Peppa Pig, A la recherche de Mr. Dinosaure (season 1, episode 2).
  Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M9FrHb4npsc, 01:57.600 to 02:56.600.

The clips and their dialogue belong to their rights holders. They are included only as short
demonstrations and are not covered by the repository's MIT licence.
