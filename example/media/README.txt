petites roues - example media

One-minute excerpts that go with the study documents in ../documents. Each clip was cut from
the source below and re-encoded small (H.264/AAC MP4, 480x270, mono audio); document timings
start at the clip's zero. The originals were not changed.

  Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mh_GJPIaPTI, 00:30.720 to 01:33.540.

peppa-pig/s01e02-a-la-recherche-de-mr-dinosaure.mp4
  Peppa Pig, A la recherche de Mr. Dinosaure (season 1, episode 2), French.
  Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M9FrHb4npsc, 01:57.600 to 02:56.600.

peppa-pig/s01e34-wu-can.zh.mp4
  Peppa Pig, 午餐 / Lunch (season 1, episode 34), Mandarin.
  Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aw6oz_WuEHg, 01:10.900 to 02:05.400.
  The Chinese text follows that upload's hand-made Simplified Chinese captions.

  Masha and the Bear, Bienenalarm! (episode 83), German.
