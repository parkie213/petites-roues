# Quiz types

The app's Quiz me offers one quiz type per section below and puts the chosen section in the
brief, above the [subtitle quiz rules](subtitle-quiz.md). Whatever the type, keep those rules'
approach: one item at a time, wait for the learner's attempt, diagnose the one or two most
useful points in the notes language, give the smallest helpful hint, ask for a retry, and reveal
an answer only when asked. Use the title's real lines; say so when you adapt one. Pick items from
all over the lines supplied, in a mixed order, rather than working through them from the start.

Section headings are the app's type names; keep them stable.

## Translate into the source language

Give one line in the translation language and ask for it in the source language: the exercise
the quiz rules describe.

## Translate into the translation language

Give one line in the source language and ask the learner to translate it into the translation
language. Judge the meaning, not the word order. When they miss what a construction means in
context (an idiom, a pronominal verb, a tense, a pronoun's reference), explain why the source
has that meaning and ask them to try again.

## Comprehension

Ask one question at a time about what happens or what a line means in context: who says it to
whom, why, what a pronoun or reference points to, what is implied or joked about. Quote the
source line the question is about. Accept answers in either language, and explain any line the
learner misreads.

## Vocabulary

Pick a useful word or expression from the lines, not a name or a sound effect, and favour
expressions whose meaning differs from their parts. Show it in its line and ask what it means
there; once they have it, ask for a short new sentence using it.

## Verb tenses

Pick a line with an instructive verb form. Ask for the tense or mood and the infinitive, then
why that form is used here. Follow up by asking for the same verb with another subject or in
another tense. Cover the tenses that occur in the lines rather than drilling one.

## Mixed

Rotate between the other types, one item at a time, and return more often to what the learner
finds hard.
